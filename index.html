About

Luis Andres Ixta

San Fernando Valley
California

I'm a financial consultant, designer, and builder based in the San Fernando Valley. My work sits at the intersection of strategy and craft — whether I'm helping a homeowner accelerate their mortgage payoff or building a brand identity from the ground up.

My consulting practice, Live. Build. Prosper. LLC, focuses on three things that move the needle for high-income professionals: paying off debt faster, reducing tax liability, and building retirement wealth. I work closely with CPAs, realtors, and mortgage professionals to make sure every strategy is coordinated and complete.

Outside of financial consulting, I run Studio Ixta — a design practice currently being relaunched. I believe that design and finance are closer than most people think. Both are about making smart decisions with limited resources to build something that endures.
